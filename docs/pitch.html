Gamuda Hackathon · Challenge 2: AI for Education & Learning

CikguAI: every student at their own pace, every teacher sees who needs help.

A bilingual (Bahasa Melayu + English) Socratic AI tutor, adaptive practice engine and teacher's assistant for Malaysian secondary schools, starting with KSSM Form 2 Mathematics and Science.

challenge2.rohaizat.cloud · one-click “Try as Student” / “Try as Teacher”

The problem

41%of Malaysian 15-year-olds reached baseline maths proficiency in PISA 2022 (OECD average 69%).
397Malaysia's PISA 2025 maths score, down another 11 points.
1 : 30One teacher, thirty students, thirty different gaps, and marking and planning that eat the time to close them.

For students (mobile-first)

  • Socratic AI tutor: hints and questions, not answers. Snap a photo of your working and it finds the exact wrong step.
  • Adaptive quizzes: each question targets your weakest skill at ~70% predicted success.
  • “Your next 3 things to study”, with reasons (weak, recent mistakes, unlocks the next topic).
  • XP, streaks, badges, daily goal and a weekly class board that rewards effort.

For teachers

  • AI-assisted marking: rubric-based draft grades with evidence, feedback and confidence. Nothing is released until the teacher approves.
  • Class insights: mastery heatmap, at-risk students, repeated misconceptions, suggested groups, AI weekly summary.
  • Lesson planner: KSSM-aligned plans built around the class's own data; edit, export to Word/PDF.
  • Worksheets: bilingual questions with answer keys; approve the best into the adaptive bank.

The demo loop: from one wrong answer to a targeted lesson

  1. Aiman gets a factorisation question wrong (sign error).
  2. The tutor diagnoses the gap, Socratically.
  3. Adaptive quiz targets it at the right difficulty.
  4. Mastery rises live: 12% → 39% → 73%.
  5. Teacher sees the 8-student sign-error cluster.
  6. One click: a remedial lesson plan for that class.

How AI and ML are used

  • MLBayesian Knowledge Tracing estimates mastery of 13 KSSM skills after every answer.
  • MLElo/Rasch ratings calibrate question difficulty from real answers and pick the next question.
  • DataWrong options are tagged with the misconception they reveal; the dashboard counts repeated ones.
  • ClaudeTutoring (streamed), photo diagnosis, rubric grading, class summaries, lesson plans and question generation, all with schema-validated output.

Responsible by design

  • Teachers approve every grade and every AI-written question before students see it.
  • Mastery never depends on the AI; scores are clamped and totalled in code.
  • Students are pseudonymised before class data is sent to the AI; photos are stripped of GPS and stored privately.
  • Teachers see tutor use per topic, not the chats. Methodology and limitations are public.
