# Future improvements

1. **More forms and subjects.** Extend from 13 Form 2 skills to Forms 1–5 and SPM subjects (Add Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Bahasa Melayu, English). The skill graph, bank format and engine already support it.
2. **Fit the models on real data.** Fit BKT parameters per skill (expectation-maximisation) and consider models with forgetting or related skills, then measure learning gains with a pilot school against a comparison class.
3. **Voice tutoring.** Speak to CikguAI in BM or English, which helps younger and less confident readers.
4. **Parent portal.** A weekly plain-language summary for parents (in BM, English, Chinese or Tamil) with one suggested activity at home.
5. **Offline-first for rural schools.** A PWA that caches the next 20 adaptive questions and syncs attempts later, so practice works on patchy prepaid data.
6. **School system integration.** Single sign-on and roster sync with school platforms (e.g. Google Classroom), plus export of assessment results.
7. **Essay and language feedback.** Rubric-based feedback for BM and English writing (karangan / essays), with the same teacher-approval workflow.
8. **Richer assessment.** Multi-question assignments, photo answers with handwritten diagrams, item analysis per question, and moderation (a second teacher spot-checks a sample of AI-drafted grades).
9. **Teacher community bank.** Share approved questions and lesson plans between schools, rated by teachers, with calibrated difficulty from real answers.
10. **Wellbeing and safety.** A counsellor escalation path for worrying messages (with clear consent rules), and PDPA-aligned data retention controls per school.
